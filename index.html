Find the nearest toilet, fast.

Common questions

A toilet is missing, closed or in the wrong place

Open the toilet in the app and tap Report an issue. To add a missing toilet, use the Add tab. New toilets appear on the map once three other people have confirmed them.

How do accessibility and RADAR key details work?

Each toilet's page asks whether the entrance is step-free, whether there's an accessible toilet (and if it needs a RADAR key), and whether there are standard toilets too. The most common answer is shown. In Profile you can say if you need an accessible toilet, need step-free access or have a RADAR key, and the NOW button will take that into account. These settings stay on your phone.

Why does it say “Hours unknown”?

Many toilets don't have opening hours recorded yet. Tissue only shows Open or Closed when it knows, so it never sends you somewhere that's shut.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and data. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

How is my location used?

Only to find toilets near you, and, if you turn on nearby alerts, to let you know about new toilets close by. It's never sold or shared for advertising. See our privacy policy for full details.
