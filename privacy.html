Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version

  • We use your location to show toilets near you. If you turn on nearby alerts, we also keep your most recent location (not a history) so we can tell you about new toilets close by.
  • Your accessibility settings (like needing an accessible toilet or having a RADAR key) stay on your phone. They're never sent to us.
  • We don't sell your data, show adverts or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.

1. Who we are

Tissue is a toilet-finding app run by Jamie Wain (“we”, “us”, “our”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Questions or requests: hello@gettissue.co.uk.

2. What we collect and why

Without an account

  • Your location, while you're using the app, to find and sort nearby toilets. It's used to look up toilets and isn't stored against you.

If you create an account

  • Email address and password, to sign you in. Passwords are stored securely (hashed) by our database provider; we can't see them.
  • Display name (optional). Used in invites you send. It may be visible to other users alongside things you contribute.
  • Toilets you add: the location, name, facilities, opening hours, access details and notes you enter.
  • Ratings and reviews. Review text is shown to other users.
  • Verifications, accessibility answers and issue reports, such as confirming a toilet exists, saying whether it's step-free or needs a RADAR key, or reporting it closed. Other users only see combined totals, never who answered.

If you turn on nearby alerts

  • A push notification token for your device, so we can send you alerts.
  • Your most recent location, including in the background. With your permission, the app updates your last known location even when it's closed, so we can tell you when a new toilet is submitted near you (within about 250 metres) and ask you to help verify it. Each update replaces the previous one: we don't keep a location history. You can turn this off at any time in your phone's Settings (Location → Tissue) or by turning off notifications.

Stored only on your phone

  • Your preferences: needing an accessible toilet, step-free access, a RADAR key, baby changing, cleanliness priority, and similar settings. These are used on your phone to choose the best toilet for you and are not sent to us.
  • A temporary copy of recently loaded toilets, so the app opens quickly.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run Tissue:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and server functions, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Privacy policy
  • Expo: app updates and delivering push notifications, together with Apple's and Google's notification services. Expo is based in the US; transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards. Privacy policy
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API: most toilet data comes from OpenStreetMap and is stored on our own database. If you're somewhere our database doesn't cover (outside the UK and Europe), the app sends your approximate location to a public Overpass server to look up nearby toilets. No account or personal details are sent with it. © OpenStreetMap contributors
  • Netlify: hosts our invite web page. Invite links include the display name of the person who sent them.
  • Apple Maps or Google Maps: when you tap “Take me there”, your maps app opens and its own privacy policy applies.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data and your contributions are kept while your account is open.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). This permanently deletes your account, profile, ratings, reviews, toilets you added, verifications, accessibility answers, push token and stored location.
  • Issue reports you made are kept to keep toilet information accurate, but are no longer linked to you.
  • Toilets already confirmed by the community may stay on the map without any link to you.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@gettissue.co.uk and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, though we'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to our database is restricted, and each user can only change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Children

Tissue isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if anything changes and tell you in the app or by email about significant changes. The latest version is always at gettissue.co.uk/privacy.
